# FormaBill — launch copy

## One-liner

Publication-grade invoices in under 60 seconds. For independent designers. Free until money moves.

## Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Invoicing that looks like your work, not like accounting software.

**First comment**

Independent designers still send invoices from a Google Doc and a PayPal/UPI request. FreshBooks and HoneyBook are built for agencies.

FormaBill is a 60-second invoice: from, bill-to, line items, tax, a live paper preview, a public link, a PDF, and Get Paid via Razorpay (India-first, not Stripe).

Free for unlimited invoices. Pro ($11/mo) is recurring, reminders, and no branding.

Would love notes from anyone who still dreads sending an invoice.

## Indie Hackers

Built a local-first invoicing tool for 1–3 person design studios.

- IndexedDB on the designer’s machine
- Public share page for the client
- Razorpay payment links (paise, UPI, cards)
- No time tracking, no proposals, no seats

Monetization is gateway fees + optional Pro. Free until money actually moves.
